# Full-size screenshots (Kiron 1.2.0)

The originals behind `public/shots/`, which holds 640×1391 Lanczos copies. All are 1320×2868 (iPhone 6.9"), from a
**Release** build of `release/1.2.0` at `cf2abc1` on the iOS simulator, with the status bar at 9:41, full bars and
battery. Every screen was reached by tapping in the app, never by a deep link. Data comes from two demo accounts on a
local Firebase emulator: no production data and no real person. Strava is hidden.

- **lifter.iphone** (the App Store set's account): Wave Loading Strength on Mon/Wed/Fri, 27 workouts since Sep 16,
  10 weeks in a row, and a ski day the day before, read from Apple Health.
- **lifter.web** (a new user who answered the new-user questions): build muscle, 1 to 3 years, a full gym, 4 days a
  week, whenever I can. She started Upper / Lower Muscle and imported 12 weeks of Strong history.
